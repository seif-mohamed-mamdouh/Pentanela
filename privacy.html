Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how Pentanela handles information in connection with this public website, business inquiries, seller account management services, and any future authorized software integration. The SP-API platform described on our website is in development and is not yet a production service.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, business name, email address, phone number, message and related business information. If you engage us for seller management services, we may receive product catalogs, SKUs, listing data, operational records and information you authorize us to access. Our website does not currently offer an Amazon seller OAuth login.

Purposes and authorized use

We use business information to answer inquiries, provide agreed services, support clients and operate the website. If we launch SP-API integration, access will require each seller's authorization and will be limited to the approved purposes and permissions needed for that seller's services. We do not use client seller data for unrelated purposes or sell it.

Storage and protection

Information is stored using the business communication and hosting services supporting our operations. We restrict access to people who need information to perform agreed duties and work to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration and loss. Before processing production SP-API data, our technical environment and security controls must be implemented and verified against Amazon's applicable requirements. The public website's HTTPS connection alone does not establish SP-API security compliance.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell business or Amazon Information. We may use hosting, email, professional support and security service providers to perform necessary services under appropriate protections. We may also disclose information where required by law. Our actual providers and disclosures will be reviewed and reflected in our developer registration and client agreements before handling production SP-API data.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and business records only as long as reasonably necessary for the reason collected, client commitments, security needs, or applicable legal obligations. For any future SP-API integration, we will document applicable retention limits and remove seller data when no longer needed or after authorization ends, subject to legal and Amazon policy requirements. Records retained for a legal reason will be protected and limited to that purpose.

Deletion, access and questions

Clients may ask us to review, correct or delete information associated with their business by emailing pentanela.egypt@gmail.com. We will assess requests according to the applicable contract, law, security considerations and Amazon policies, and explain any legal reason information must be retained. Sellers will also be able to revoke authorization in Amazon's systems when an integration becomes available.

Website technology and third-party services

Our public pages load fonts supplied by Google Fonts and are hosted by a third-party website provider. Those providers may receive technical connection data such as IP addresses according to their policies. If analytics or additional cookies are added in the future, this policy and any required consent mechanism will be updated.

Regional services

Pentanela operates from Egypt and supports businesses interested in the Egypt, Saudi Arabia and UAE marketplaces. Where personal information is involved, we will consider applicable local privacy obligations before expanding processing activities.

Policy updates and contact

We will post meaningful revisions to this page and update the date above. For privacy questions and requests, contact pentanela.egypt@gmail.com.
